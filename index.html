About me

I'm a mechanical engineering student who likes the whole journey of a product: sketching the idea, modelling it in 3D, testing it, and presenting it well.

My work centres on mechanical design and 3D CAD in Autodesk Fusion, Inventor and Onshape. I also build robotics projects and write software to control and automate them. Alongside engineering, I design posters and visuals that make technical ideas easy to understand.
